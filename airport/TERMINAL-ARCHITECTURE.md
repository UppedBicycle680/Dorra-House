# Terminal architecture references — 9 September 2026

`terminal-architecture.mjs` replaces the shared striped terminal roof with airport-specific miniature architecture. Ground footprints use the airport diagrams listed in REFERENCES.md; heights, facade bays, individual solar panels and membrane folds are visual interpretations. The cream/teal palette and game economy are retained.

- **YBSU:** [Airport owner Palisade's existing-terminal photograph](https://www.palisadegroup.com/assets/sunshine-coast-airport/) (`SCA-2020-scaled.jpeg`) shows low white roofs, solar arrays and a peaked landside entrance canopy. These features sit on the H116/26 footprint. The operator's [terminal redevelopment page](https://www.sunshinecoastairport.com.au/corporate/projects-and-planning/terminal-redevelopment/) identifies its images as future architectural renders; they are not used as an existing building reference.
- **Gold Coast:** [Queensland Airports Limited's aerial](https://www.qldairports.com.au/about/gold-coast-airport) distinguishes the long low hall from the taller southern expansion with a dark apron-facing glazed facade. The reference aerial's roof service strips inform restrained detailing on the diagram footprint.
- **Brisbane:** [BAC's circular lounge photograph](https://www.bne.com.au/blog/people-bne/people-bne-andrew-gott-project-manager) shows a shallow radial roof with a raised glazed drum. The [domestic skylight project](https://www.bne.com.au/corporate/projects/completed-projects/domestic-terminal-skylight-upgrade) confirms the continuous curved skylight. The [2025 parking location map](https://www.bne.com.au/sites/default/files/docs/2025-04/Airport_Parking_Options_Map_Apr2025_1.pdf) identifies the two domestic landside blocks as P2/P1 car parks, and the separate international landside block as P1. They were incorrectly labelled as extra terminal halls; their chart footprints are retained as parking structures, with duplicate domestic decks removed. Internal obstacle IDs remain stable. [BAC's solar project](https://www.bne.com.au/corporate/projects/completed-projects/airport-wide-rooftop-solar) and the [international aerial](https://brisbaneopenhouse.com.au/brisbanes-international-terminal-a-journey-through-history/) inform grouped arrays. Parking structures do not act as passenger-terminal click targets.
- **Archerfield:** The [operator Master Plan](https://archerfieldairport.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Masterplan2022.pdf) precinct diagram identifies Terminal **28** southwest of Hangar 6. This corresponds to AD path **21454**, not Hangar 6/path 21455 or office/path 21432. [Brisbane City Council's heritage record](https://heritage.brisbane.qld.gov.au/heritage-places/144) and the [terminal photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Archerfield_Terminal,_Brisbane_QLD_Australia_(7469742402).jpg) establish the stepped three-storey centre, low wings and observation-deck railing. The former rooftop control cab is omitted.
- **Hamilton Island:** The previously photo-informed pitched modules remain; the generic glass stripes beneath them are removed. See the Hamilton realism sources in REFERENCES.md.
- **Redcliffe:** A low clubhouse roof and broad glazing replace the generic terminal treatment. Its detailed club precinct refinement shares the dated council aerial registration; see `redcliffe-reference.mjs` and `redcliffe-club.mjs`. The terminal remains selectable without an overlapping invisible tower target; management upgrades remain available in Build.

Screenshot rounds, the mismatch ledger and isolated game-interface checks are retained outside the repository in the terminal-realism QA artifact. These checks establish the documented miniature approximation, not surveyed architectural accuracy or current construction-site conditions.

The `terminals` layout collection historically also carries chart structures such as GA hangars. Its three Brisbane `kind: 'parking'` entries are retained there to preserve the ground-route obstacle identities. Rendering distinguishes their purpose and excludes them from terminal hit targets.

The dedicated YBSU follow-up replaces the single clipped roof peak with a separate landside tensile entrance and refines the roof, frontage and drop-off. See YBSU-TERMINAL.md for the current sources, implementation and QA record.
