# Archerfield airport-wide hangar inventory

Reviewed 9 September 2026. The model now contains 78 individually placed buildings: 71 named hangar sites and seven associated aviation buildings. This extends the earlier seven-hangar model. Site numbers identify buildings/tenancies, not a count of independent roof structures or operational businesses. LifeFlight 409 has four bays within one modeled complex.

The airport property page describes 70 hangars; the July 2026 tenant handbook describes 72 hangars and aeroport sites. Those totals use different categories. This inventory preserves all identified site footprints rather than fabricating or removing a building to force a matching total. Current use of converted workshops and shared roof tenancies can differ from their site numbers.

Each entry has its own footprint, frontage, roof geometry and material data. Heights, exact panel spacing, most door details and hidden interiors remain estimates; this is not a survey or a certification of visually identical facades. See ARCHERFIELD-ALL-HANGARS-QA.md for the comparison and checks.
